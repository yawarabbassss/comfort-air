# Comfort-Air Engineering & Primo Plumbing Website Redesign

Modern, high-converting, and SEO-optimized website redesign for **Comfort-Air Engineering, Inc. & Primo Plumbing, Inc.** (San Antonio, TX — Established 1964).

## 🚀 Features

- **Signature Hero Section:** High-impact typography, glassmorphic floating trust pills, average rating widget, and instant repair dispatch card.
- **Continuous Right-to-Left Ticker:** Infinite smooth moving marquee highlighting 24/7 service guarantees, reviews, and credentials.
- **Why Choose Us Grid:** Asymmetrical layout featuring licensed technician profiles and customer satisfaction guarantees.
- **Customer Video & Quote Testimonials:** 3-column masonry grid combining customer review quotes with video stories and pulsating triggers.
- **Priority Heating & Cooling Banner:** Split dark layout with direct emergency hotline `(210) 494-1691` and lead capture form.
- **Precision Performance & Perfection:** Live metrics and progress statistics.
- **Reliable 3-Step Process:** Complete installation and maintenance workflow breakdown.
- **Interactive Review Showcase:** High-impact yellow review section with horizontal carousel controls.
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion:** Expandable common questions and answers.
- **Luxury Multi-Column Footer:** Comprehensive services breakdown, license credentials (`TACLA001234E`, `#M-38910`), San Antonio service area tags, and 24/7 dispatch hotline.
- **Interactive Modals & Widgets:** Sticky emergency call pill, back-to-top button, and priority booking modal.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** (Semantic SEO structure + Schema.org JSON-LD local business markup)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Custom theme configuration)
- **Lucide Icons & Vector SVGs**
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (IntersectionObserver animations, smooth scrolling, modal state management)
